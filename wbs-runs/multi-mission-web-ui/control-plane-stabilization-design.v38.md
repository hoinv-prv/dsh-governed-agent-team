## 1. Problem statement

The mission repeatedly entered a control loop:

```text
partial readiness check
  → charge attempt
  → mutate one or more control artifacts
  → discover a missing validator, unavailable capability, authority mismatch, or stale state
  → fail review/acceptance
  → pause and re-plan
```

Product-level causes existed, especially the late split of matrix, control state, handlers/reducers and runner assembly. They do not explain why governance failures continued after that split. The best-supported common control deficiency is the absence of one fail-closed pre-charge admission protocol that jointly attests time-bounded known readiness across every authoritative control surface. The evidence shows only that such a gate would have blocked failures whose unreadiness already existed and was observable at the charge boundary; it does not prove that fragmented admission was the sole cause of every historical failure or that a successful availability snapshot predicts later provider, sandbox or external-state loss. Those post-observation losses remain charged `REVIEW_PENDING`, `INTERRUPTED_CHARGED` or terminal recovery paths.

This design stabilizes the procedural control plane before another WBS is built. It does not modify GAT product behavior, create a scheduler, replace native DSH/AIWS mechanisms, or authorize execution.

## 2. Evidence basis and root-cause classification

The evidence-bound failure model used for v32 is the immutable snapshot `wbs-runs/multi-mission-web-ui/council-reviews/control-plane-stabilization/control-plane-stabilization-v1-council-002/snapshots/failure-model`, SHA-256 `434f1613751d8a2b5b8813936404e4567e698ea4ace88ed74863e2a56e4d8b86`; the mutable Workspace path is only its working source.

### 2.1 Systemic root cause

**RC-SYSTEM-01 (best-supported common control deficiency):** admission is fragmented. WBS validation, execution-ledger validity, AIP/ASC transition, goal/Team authority, command capability, reviewer availability, ownership/quiescence and budget slack are checked by different mechanisms at different times. The observed failures map to one or more missing pre-charge predicates, so a charge can be committed while another mandatory surface is already known invalid. This is not claimed as the sole causal explanation of every historical defect.

### 2.2 Counterfactual coverage of observed failures

| Observed control failure | Pre-charge predicate that would have failed |
|---|---|
| invalid/semantically incomplete WBS revision | P1/P2 plan identity and semantic closure |
| strict execution-ledger rejection | P3 ledger strict validity |
| missing generated ASC transition command or stale ASC | P6 AIP/ASC closure |
| unavailable sandbox/effect backend or undeclared command | P7 command authorization/capability |
| stale/contradictory coordinator, Goal, Team, active work or execution-ledger run state | P8–P10 same-epoch snapshot and ledger↔goal equality |
| unavailable reviewer route or malformed review transport | P11 availability/fallback |
| no attempt slack for mandatory recovery | P12 budget safety |
| absent or misbound HUMAN authority | P13 authority |

This establishes control coverage, not exclusive historical causation and not non-bypassable runtime enforcement.

### 2.3 Contributing conditions

- product architecture was reviewed after implementation rather than before it;
- plan schema validation did not prove semantic command-to-deliverable closure;
- the mutable ledger accumulated incompatible historical representations;
- generated ASC state could be manually edited without a declared owning transition command;
- reviewer-route and sandbox capability were not observed and bound before charge;
- execution ledger and runtime goal could disagree on active/paused state;
- attempt allocation left no recovery reserve.

### 2.4 Symptoms, not root causes

- failed runner attempts;
- sandbox refusal on attempt 65;
- reviewer failure on attempt 66;
- stale or manually reconciled ASC;
- strict progress-render rejection;
- repeated selection/reconciliation attempts.

## 3. Design goals and non-goals

### 3.1 Goals

1. Make all execution readiness checks one closed, content-addressed admission decision.
2. Block charge with `EXPIRED` when drift is observed before commit; separately disclose bypass and the state-change window after the final observation as irreducible residual risks, never as permission to ignore observed drift.
3. Preserve uncertain/interrupted charges after commit; never roll them back or reuse them.
4. Prevent manual or undeclared transitions of generated control artifacts.
5. Separate immutable history from the small current execution projection.
6. Reserve review/recovery call budget and prove route availability before work begins without claiming provider capacity is held.
7. Produce deterministic evidence that a later WBS can cite literally.

### 3.2 Non-goals

- No new runtime service, daemon, scheduler, custom WBS runner, or automatic approval mechanism.
- No bypass of HUMAN decisions, native Team approval, Working AIP, Workspace, or DSH tools.
- No GAT product implementation or accepted-baseline execution.
- No rewrite of historical evidence without exact backup and a reviewed migration rule.
- No DSH checkout access, package install, network, activation, merge or deployment.

### 5.1 Critical rule

No mutable mission ledger, selected-plan state, task/product state, acceptance state or attempt charge is procedurally allowed in `FROZEN`, `PREPARING`, `NOT_READY`, `READY`, or `EXPIRED`. Within the normal admission lifecycle, the only pre-charge writes are six coordinator-owned, uncharged control/evidence effects separately approved under P15: (a) on the `FROZEN → PREPARING` edge, the exact AIP-declared `python3 .ai-work/tooling/run_aip.py start|resume|step ...` transition may update only the AIP pointer/ASC and canonical Workspace control evidence, with argv/tool hash, before/after hashes, native sequence and observation epoch recorded in the P6 receipt; (b) create-once persistence of exact sealed `AdmissionBundle` bytes at the content-addressed canonical bundle path; (c) append of one immutable `AdmissionInvalidation` through the approved append operation; and (d) ordered create-once content-addressed persistence of the exact pre-write execution-source bytes, strictly validated candidate bytes and inline fresh P14/boundary receipt plus recomputable raw observation bytes immediately before charge, followed by a separate version-guarded `TransitionIntentIndex` OPEN append, through exact P15-approved seal/index argv and receipts; and (e) ordered create-once persistence of a `NOT_APPLIED` `ExecutionTransitionCompletion` for an abandoned pre-charge intent followed by a separate version-guarded matching index CLOSE append, through exact P15-bound completion/index argv, content-addressed paths, receipts and validators. Effect (e) is coordinator-owned, may reference only its existing intent, must strict-validate and is charged to the common administrative ledger's pre-charge-admission allocation; it cannot write `execution.json`, allocate/consume an attempt, change charge count, create APPLIED, or mutate task/product/acceptance state, and differing existing bytes or persistence failure leaves `NOT_READY` pending HUMAN-authorized recovery; and (f) guarded append(s) to the canonical `AdministrativeResourceLedger`, with the append receipt fields embedded atomically in each entry/hash, only through the exact P15-bound argv/path/effect and reservation described in Section 6.3. Effect (f) runs first to reserve the admission envelope and then only to record operations against that reservation; append/receipt mismatch or failure enters `ADMISSION_ACCOUNTING_BLOCKED`, permits no mission-ledger/task write, and exits only through fresh HUMAN-authorized administrative-ledger recovery under the same reservation/ceiling. None may allocate/consume an attempt or mutate mission-ledger/product/acceptance bytes; the AIP transition is limited to declared pointer/ASC/Workspace-control paths, bundle sealing cannot overwrite, and the invalidation operation may change only its canonical append-only index. `READY` is not authority to execute; it only attests that the exact admission bundle was complete at its recorded hashes/epochs. Every live predicate must be re-observed immediately at the charge boundary; any drift expires the bundle. Two administrative states remain outside normal admission: Section 6.3 `BOOTSTRAP_MAINTENANCE` may write only its HUMAN-bounded schema/tool/evidence paths and Section 9 `MIGRATION_MAINTENANCE` is the sole ledger-write exception before valid P3. Neither is READY/COMMITTING or may allocate a mission attempt; bootstrap must return exact HUMAN-accepted capability hashes, and migration must return an accepted migrated-base hash or exact restore proof, to `FROZEN` before admission begins.

### 5.1.1 Serialization and hash-domain profile

Every accepted schema/validator pins one profile: RFC 8785 JSON Canonicalization Scheme; UTF-8 without BOM; no Unicode normalization beyond the code points already present; SHA-256 over exact serialized raw bytes; digests represented as 64 lowercase ASCII hexadecimal characters. Structured multi-value domains use canonical JSON arrays, never ambiguous string concatenation. Content-addressed object paths are always derived out-of-band as `<typed-root>/<raw_object_sha256>.json` and are excluded from the object bytes/hash unless an explicitly earlier logical ID is referenced. Validators reject alternate encodings, casing, aliases, normalization or framing.

### 5.2 Commit semantics

Cross-file atomicity is not claimed. The commit protocol is a logical write-ahead transaction:

1. Execute P14 in full immediately before commit: resolve the canonical `AcceptedDesignRegistry` latest sole-current generation and require exact generation/design/HUMAN-acceptance/authority-basis/ReviewClosureReceipt equality to the bundle (any superseding append expires it); re-hash every bundle input and every P13/P15/P16 authority/delegation record; on first post-migration charge re-hash every `migration_authority` lineage link; re-check current scope, actor/owner chain and revocation for each; and re-read the canonical invalidation index under its sealed prefix-extension rule, predicate scope and comparison basis of native sequence plus recorded epoch.
2. Under that identical rule/scope/basis, confirm exact HUMAN WBS and active goal/Team authority still match and re-observe every live P7–P11 predicate. Any missing/unavailable/drifted/revoked authority, scope or migration-lineage link takes the uncharged abort branch; a timestamp window never overrides changed native state.
3. **Abort branch:** if steps 1–2 do not return fresh PASS, the live check uses a different/missing rule, epoch, sequence or scope, observation is unavailable, or any value drifts, perform no durable mission mutation, consume no attempt ID, use only the approved invalidation-append operation to append one immutable uncharged `AdmissionInvalidation`, and transition the candidate to `EXPIRED`/`NOT_READY` before re-prepare. If that append fails, remain `NOT_READY`, perform no ledger/task write, record the unavailable evidence outside mission controls, and require HUMAN/runtime repair before another prepare.
4. Before this mechanism may exist, a separate HUMAN-authorized execution-contract decision must choose exactly one proven representation: **(a)** exact existing `dsh-wbs-execution/1` JSON pointers for attempt allocation, cumulative charge, bundle hash, seal hash, boundary-receipt hash, lineage and attempt ID, accompanied by a strict-validator-passing fixture/hash; or **(b)** exact bytes/hash of a versioned execution-contract extension plus validator and migration accepted under Section 9. If neither is proven, P15 is `NOT_READY`; no field is invented. Using only the selected representation, construct an in-memory candidate template without claiming a final boundary receipt; do not seal or write it yet.
5. **Preliminary boundary construction with acyclic commitment:** first derive `candidate_attempt_id = "attempt-" + first32hex(SHA256(JCS([mission_id,plan_hash,task_id,source_hash,source_version,source_attempt_count+1])))`; derive `idempotency_key = SHA256(JCS([mission_id,plan_hash,task_id,edge,source_hash,source_version,previous_completion_hash,previous_completion_sequence,candidate_attempt_id]))`; then derive `logical_intent_id = SHA256(JCS([mission_id, plan_hash, source_hash, source_version, previous_completion_hash, previous_completion_sequence, edge, candidate_attempt_id, idempotency_key]))`. `first32hex` means the first 32 lowercase hexadecimal characters of the 64-hex SHA-256 result; all inputs are the exact typed UTF-8/JCS values named here, and collision/duplicate with any existing attempt ID is `NOT_READY`. `candidate_attempt_id` is only a deterministic proposed identifier in pre-charge evidence; it is not allocated, counted or consumed until the guarded ledger write persists the exact candidate. Next derive `candidate_template_hash = SHA256(JCS(candidate_tree_without_preflight))`, where `candidate_tree_without_preflight` is the complete strict-schema candidate JSON tree with exactly the selected execution-contract pointer `/charge_boundary_preflight_sha256` absent—not null, sentinel or placeholder—and every other schema path/value present; candidate/self hashes and undeclared fields are forbidden. Read `raw_source_attempt_ids` exactly from the source ledger in source order and reject `NOT_READY` before hashing if any element is non-string, empty or duplicated; only after that PASS define `source_attempt_ids = raw_source_attempt_ids` without normalization as the UTF-8 attempt-ID array in source-ledger order and `candidate_attempt_ids` as that exact array with `candidate_attempt_id` appended once. Their digest preimages are exactly UTF-8 bytes of RFC 8785 JCS for those arrays; the empty preimage is exactly `[]`; `source_attempt_ids_sha256` and `candidate_attempt_ids_sha256` are lowercase 64-hex SHA-256 of those bytes. Define `attempt_invariants` as the closed JCS object `{source_attempt_ids_sha256,source_attempt_count,source_attempts_charged,candidate_attempt_id,candidate_attempt_ids_sha256,candidate_attempt_count,candidate_attempts_charged}`. Define `administrative_ids` as a closed JCS object whose keys are exactly `writer_ids`, `authorization_ids`, `outcome_ids`, and `bounded_retry_pair_ids`; each value is a duplicate-free UTF-8 string array sorted lexicographically by raw Unicode code-point sequence, with empty encoded as `[]`. Reject duplicate IDs, non-string IDs, omitted keys, extra keys or a non-canonical order. Define `intent_body_domain` exactly as the closed JCS object `{logical_intent_id,source_hash,source_version,previous_completion_hash,previous_completion_sequence,edge,attempt_invariants,reservation_id,administrative_ids,candidate_template_hash}`; then and only then compute `PlannedIntentBodyCommitment = SHA256(JCS(intent_body_domain))`. The domain excludes the body commitment itself, preflight bytes/hash, final candidate bytes/hash, final intent bytes/hash, content-addressed intent path, and OPEN bytes/hash/sequence. Perform full P14/live-authority/invalidation and P7–P12 re-observation, then emit immutable `ChargeBoundaryPreflightReceipt` binding that body commitment and the zero-open index prior hash/sequence. The preflight authorizes only one later OPEN whose referenced final intent projects exactly to `intent_body_domain`. Embed the unchanged preflight hash in the candidate; never recompute it.
6. Deterministically construct/strict-validate candidate from template plus preflight hash; construct the final intent by adding preflight and candidate bytes/hashes to the committed body; validate that applying exactly the named committed-body projection recomputes `PlannedIntentBodyCommitment`; then derive `final_intent_hash = SHA256(final_intent_bytes)` and content-addressed path `<intent-root>/<final_intent_hash>.json`; the path is never an input to that hash. Derive the exact OPEN entry/new index hash/sequence only from `logical_intent_id`, final hash/path and prior index. Persist/re-read/validate intent and append only that OPEN. Inside the once-authorized writer, accept only exact preflight-member equality, permitted invalidation/root/admin prefix extensions, and prior index plus exactly that single OPEN whose intent projection matches the preflight commitment; reject every other index change. Validate root/admin, final intent, OPEN, reservation and `P3_IN_FLIGHT`. Post-invocation `GuardedWriteReceipt` binds preflight, body commitment, final intent/candidate, prior/new index bytes/hashes/sequences, observations and result. A byte-exact construction fixture must prove the ordered derivation `body commitment → preflight → candidate → final intent → OPEN`; no member hashes a later object through an included domain. The irreducible post-read race remains disclosed.
7. After persisted-byte equality and strict PASS, enter `CHARGED_ACCOUNTING_PENDING` and execute the Section 6.3 charged accounting/completion/index-close order. Only after validated exact-writer `GuardedWriteReceipt`, its closed authorization/outcome pair, the completion authorization, create-once APPLIED `ExecutionTransitionCompletion` and matching outcome, the index-close authorization, matching CLOSE/receipt and outcome, final reservation reconciliation, and ordinary P3 with zero open intents may the coordinator apply declared task mutations and dispatch. If persisted equality or strict validation fails, dispatch nothing and enter only `TRANSITION_RECOVERY_BLOCKED_DIVERGENT`; no `POST_CHARGE_REPAIR_*` alternative exists. Preserve source/candidate/observed bytes, validator output, attempt/charge and original OPEN, then use only Section 9.1 `DIVERGENT_CURRENT_TERMINAL_RECOVERY`: first append the HUMAN-bound `DIVERGENT_UNKNOWN_WRITE` exceptional disposition and matching `RECOVERY_CLOSE` for the original intent, yielding a P3-recognized zero-open exceptional state; only then create the observed-current-to-`FAILED_CHARGED_PROVENANCE_UNKNOWN` recovery intent/write/completion/CLOSE. This is the sole same-attempt target and never dispatches, resumes or accepts.
8. If any later post-charge mutation or dispatch fails, use a Section 9.1 transition receipt to mark the same attempt `INTERRUPTED_CHARGED` or `FAILED_CHARGED`; preserve its charge and evidence. Never return to `READY` using that attempt ID.

`INTERRUPTED_CHARGED` is procedurally owned by the coordinator but authorizes no dispatch by itself. A fresh attributable HUMAN interruption-disposition record must bind the same attempt/charge, bundle/seal/boundary hashes, interruption evidence, exact remaining commands/effects/resources, resume-count ceiling, designated-owner/delegation chain and either `resume_same_attempt` or `close_failed`. For resume, the coordinator revalidates current dependencies/source hashes, P7–P11 capabilities/state and P13/P15 scope/revocation; any drift beyond the original bound effects forbids resume. A valid decision plus PASS uses a named `interrupted_to_dispatched` `ExecutionTransitionIntent`/`ExecutionTransitionCompletion` pair and dispatches the same attempt without a charge/allocation change. `close_failed` uses `interrupted_to_failed`; unavailable/invalid authority or failed revalidation remains `INTERRUPTED_CHARGED`. Exhausted resume count requires HUMAN closure as failed; no implicit retry/new attempt.

The design detects known unreadiness before charge; it does not pretend failures after a legitimate charge can be undone.

### 5.3 Enforcement boundary and residual race

No existing pinned Source of Truth identifies a non-bypassable native charge entry point that consumes an AdmissionBundle. Therefore this design claims **procedural fail-closed governance**, not hard runtime enforcement. The coordinator must bind the bundle hash to the immediately following charge record and re-observe live goal, Team, worker/job, sandbox and reviewer-availability predicates at that boundary. An irreducible check-to-charge race remains when external state can change between observation and mutation. A delegated or otherwise capable writer may also bypass the procedural single-coordinator path because no native capability denial is established; P8 observes topology/quiescence but does not prove such a writer is technically unable to charge.

Hard prevention of bypass or a true compare-and-swap charge requires a separately approved DSH/runtime change and is outside this design. Until such a capability exists, any path that cannot demonstrate coordinator compliance and boundary re-observation is `NOT_READY`; the residual enforcement/race risk must be visible to the HUMAN in the WBS approval packet.

### 5.4 Acceptance boundary

A validator-backed `ReviewClosureReceipt` PASS with verdict `pass` or `pass_with_notes` never implies acceptance; it only moves the charged attempt to `AWAITING_HUMAN_ACCEPTANCE`. The acceptance owner must be `actor.kind = human`—either the designated mission decision owner or a HUMAN actor reached through a complete hash-pinned, non-revoked HUMAN delegation chain from that owner with exact task/scope at every link—and must provide a fresh attributable decision whose raw record binds the exact mission/WBS/task/attempt, output and verifier hashes, review/report hashes, accept-or-reject action, scope and current P16 owner/delegation record plus revocation state. A non-HUMAN delegate is invalid even if some HUMAN record names it. Missing, stale, ambiguous, non-HUMAN or revoked authority enters coordinator-owned `ACCEPTANCE_PENDING`, which authorizes no acceptance write. A fresh/superseding HUMAN owner/delegation and accept-or-reject decision revalidated under P16 returns to `AWAITING_HUMAN_ACCEPTANCE` for projection; without it the state remains pending. Timeout/silence cannot exit.

The coordinator may only project that exact decision through the HUMAN-approved acceptance operation declared in the WBS closure matrix and Section 9.1 transition protocol. The first prevalidated, guarded receipt-linked write may produce only unaccepted `ACCEPTANCE_VERIFYING`, binding the decision/action, current ledger, exact deterministic final candidate bytes/hash and all output/verifier/review-closure hashes; dependencies still treat it as unaccepted. An independent verifier checks the persisted pending record, live P16 authority and exact proposed final bytes/hash. `confirmed` produces a bound verification receipt but does not itself authorize a stale later write. Immediately at every final accept/reject write boundary—including a source-equal intent restart—the coordinator re-hashes the decision-bound output, verifier, `ReviewClosureReceipt`, HUMAN decision, complete owner-rooted delegation/revocation records and current ledger source/version, and revalidates P16 plus exact equality to the verified pending/candidate hashes. Only PASS permits the second pre-intended guarded transition to exactly `ACCEPTED` or `REJECTED_CHARGED`; persisted bytes are re-hashed and strict-validated. Drift, revocation or unavailable observation detected before the ledger write produces no final bytes and transitions only through a separately pre-intended unaccepted edge to `ACCEPTANCE_PENDING` for authority/decision drift or `ACCEPTANCE_VERIFICATION_PENDING` for evidence/verification drift. No native primitive is proven to atomically compare-and-set both `execution.json` and the external authority/revocation source, so revocation may still race the last read and persist a stale final projection. `refuted` enters `ACCEPTANCE_VERIFICATION_FAILED`; unavailable/inconclusive enters `ACCEPTANCE_VERIFICATION_PENDING`. Those states authorize no final projection; a new independent verification over identical inputs may return to `ACCEPTANCE_VERIFYING`, while changed bytes/authority require `ACCEPTANCE_PENDING` and a fresh HUMAN decision. No HUMAN/coordinator/reviewer waiver can replace confirmation. Exact reject preserves the attempt/evidence.

Every final projection receipt therefore binds the observed authority/revocation canonical path, hash, native sequence and epoch. Immediately after persisted equality/validation, and again immediately before any dependency satisfaction, activation, downstream dispatch or mission closure that would rely on `ACCEPTED`, the coordinator re-reads P16 and requires exact equality to that receipt plus continued non-revocation. A mismatch or unavailable observation makes the persisted projection non-usable and enters `ACCEPTANCE_AUTHORITY_CONFLICT` through a predeclared transition; this state has unaccepted dependency semantics and permits only a fresh owner-rooted HUMAN decision plus full two-phase acceptance verification, never reuse of the stale projection. Each reliance check has the same disclosed irreducible external-state TOCTOU after its last read; this design claims procedural fail-closed checking, not atomic prevention. `REJECTED_CHARGED` remains terminal rejection evidence and never satisfies dependencies.

Acceptance projection is subordinate to the uniform Section 9.1 source/candidate/divergent recovery branches; no separate `ACCEPTANCE_PROJECTION_REPAIR_*` states exist. Source-equal with a valid intent may close NOT_APPLIED or undergo fresh edge-specific checks; candidate-equal and strict-valid may finalize the exact pre-intended edge only with its valid pre-existing guarded receipt and still-current P16/evidence checks. Any partial, strict-invalid or neither-source-nor-candidate state enters only `TRANSITION_RECOVERY_BLOCKED_DIVERGENT` and may reach only `FAILED_CHARGED_PROVENANCE_UNKNOWN`, never `ACCEPTED` or `REJECTED_CHARGED`. A stale/revoked semantic decision returns through a new pre-intended unaccepted edge to `ACCEPTANCE_PENDING`; it is never repaired into acceptance.

## 6. Immutable AdmissionBundle

Prepare performs reads and returns canonical bytes but writes nothing. Thus bundle sealing is the sole persistence effect for preparation evidence and cannot replay a mutable or unsealed receipt path. The later fresh P14/boundary observations and raw evidence are embedded in the charge-preimage/`ExecutionTransitionIntent` bytes persisted by that already-declared create-once effect.

### 6.1 Contract and operation ownership bootstrap

`AdmissionBundle` above is a **proposed design contract**, not an existing authoritative schema. Before any successor WBS is built, a separately HUMAN-authorized control-plane stabilization action must persist and approve: (a) the exact schema bytes and SHA-256; (b) a deterministic validator and its exact `python3 <approved-validator-path> validate <bundle-path>` argv; and (c) the coordinator-owned read-only prepare operation and exact `python3 <approved-prepare-path> prepare ...` argv, which computes canonical bytes without changing mission/control state; (d) the coordinator-owned create-once bundle-seal operation, content-addressed storage root, exact `python3 <approved-bundle-path> seal ...` argv, allowed-effect contract, post-write hash/schema validation and crash recovery; (e) the coordinator-owned uncharged invalidation append operation, canonical index path, exact `python3 <approved-invalidation-path> append ...` argv, allowed-effect contract, deterministic validator and recovery behavior; (f) the Section 6.3 exact one-time AdministrativeLedgerGenesis decision/persistent RootControlJournal writer/AIP/Workspace/authoring/review/lint/staging/finalizer/verifier/receipt paths, hashes, finite seed and post-genesis administrative-recovery ceilings, identities and fail-stop/recovery contract, followed by the exact AdministrativeResourceLedger contract/path/validator/coordinator-only guarded append and cumulative ceiling; (g) either exact existing `dsh-wbs-execution/1` JSON-pointer mappings plus a strict-valid charge fixture, or a HUMAN-authorized versioned execution-contract extension/validator/migration; and (h) the coordinator-owned create-once charge-preimage seal plus version-guarded charged-accounting finalization/reconciliation, provenance-terminal recovery and interrupted-disposition transition operations, exact argv/effects, immutable transition-intent/completion/guarded-write-receipt/unknown-provenance-recovery/intent-corruption-tombstone/intent-root-manifest-supersession/uncharged-completion-corruption-tombstone/uncharged-not-applied-corruption-recovery/completion-corruption-tombstone/charged-completion-corruption-recovery/post-charge-recovery-allocation/index-generation-supersession/administrative-root-authorization+candidate-commit+attempt-outcome+recovery/administrative-authorization-outcome-pair plus the exact named `DIVERGENT_UNKNOWN_WRITE`, `UNKNOWN_APPLIED_NO_RECEIPT`, completion-corruption and index-generation-supersession recovery schemas, review-route-authorization-amendment plus review-closure receipt schemas/validators, create-once/idempotent-finalize operations and P3 lineage/recovery rules. Until those real paths/hashes/commands exist and pass, admission is `NOT_READY`; this design does not invent them or claim P1/P2 mechanical validation.

Bootstrap item (i) creates the canonical `AcceptedDesignRegistry` at the HUMAN-selected path using an accepted create-once/append-only operation, closed schema and deterministic validator. Generation 0 contains exactly `{generation:0,current:true,design_path,design_raw_sha256,human_design_acceptance_id,human_design_acceptance_raw_sha256,repo_rules_sha256,aiws_architecture_sha256,previous_generation_sha256:null}`; the decision owner must separately accept the exact design bytes before the coordinator may create it. Each later generation is `prior+1`, sets exactly one entry `current=true`, binds the prior generation hash plus a fresh owner-rooted HUMAN acceptance/re-baseline record and preserves every prior entry. Bootstrap owns generation-0 creation; the coordinator owns later append under a fresh HUMAN re-baseline decision. Missing, duplicate-current, divergent, partially written, non-monotone or non-HUMAN-rooted generations enter `BOOTSTRAP_BLOCKED` before first use and `NOT_READY` thereafter; recovery requires preserved bytes, a fresh HUMAN decision, independent verification and either exact idempotent completion or a superseding accepted generation—never overwrite.

Bootstrap item (j) creates generation 0 of the canonical `TransitionCapabilityManifest` and binds its canonical path, closed schema/validator, generation-0 bytes/raw hash and HUMAN acceptance. The coordinator alone may append a create-once next generation under a fresh owner-rooted HUMAN design/manifest acceptance and a pre-bound administrative authorization/outcome pair; divergence or partial persistence enters `BOOTSTRAP_BLOCKED` before first use and `NOT_READY` thereafter, with preserved bytes and fresh HUMAN recovery required. P15 rejects an unknown writer, missing pair, generation gap, overwrite or ambiguous current generation.

Bootstrap item (k) creates generation 0 of the canonical `TransitionIntentRootManifest` at the HUMAN-selected intent-root path. Its closed schema/validator bind generation, prior-generation hash/null, every content-addressed intent/tombstone object path and raw hash, owner and HUMAN acceptance; generation 0 is the accepted empty manifest. The coordinator alone creates a next append-only generation under the named corruption-recovery authority. Missing/divergent/partial generation enters `INTENT_CORRUPT_BLOCKED`; P3/P15 re-hash the accepted current generation and reject any unaccounted object. Bootstrap and Section 16 bind the exact path, schema/validator/operation hashes, generation-0 bytes and HUMAN acceptance.

Bundle and seal identities are disjoint and out-of-band: `bundle_sha256 = SHA256(raw_bundle_bytes)` and bundle path is `<bundle-root>/<bundle_sha256>.json`; `seal_sha256 = SHA256(raw_seal_bytes)` and seal path is `<seal-root>/<seal_sha256>.json`. Neither digest is embedded in its own hashed bytes. Every field/reference is typed as bundle or seal hash; aliasing or using one path/domain for the other is invalid.

After bootstrap, the coordinator owns read-only bundle computation through the approved prepare command and exact durable sealing through the separate create-once seal command. `READY` is legal only after the complete canonical bundle bytes and validator receipt are durably stored at a path derived from `bundle_sha256`, re-read equal to the computed bytes, and independently retrievable; collision, overwrite request, missing bytes, hash/schema mismatch or seal crash leaves `NOT_READY`. A crash after seal but before charge leaves an uncharged retrievable bundle that must pass P14 or expire; a crash after charge can reconstruct proof from the charge-bound bundle hash and canonical bytes. The coordinator also owns the first durable charge/boundary mutation through one version-guarded `execution.json` update using the runtime's native guarded file write/edit operation, bounded by the HUMAN-approved WBS hash and declared write effect. A stale-version conflict, failed predicate, unavailable guard or missing authority aborts with no charge and no attempt allocation. The operation must re-observe live predicates and bind the immutable bundle full-file hash, `AdmissionBundleSeal` sidecar hash, boundary receipt and allocated attempt ID in the one ledger update. This is attributable procedural ownership, not a non-bypassable native DSH hook.

### 6.2 Immutable invalidation representation

A sealed bundle never changes. At seal time it pins the invalidation index's canonical path, raw-byte SHA-256, item count, last sequence, owner identity, observation epoch and the same freshness rule used by the bundle. An unavailable index or any mismatch makes the candidate `NOT_READY`, never `READY`.

When drift, withdrawal or failed re-observation is detected, the coordinator invokes only the HUMAN-approved invalidation append argv/effect and appends an immutable uncharged `AdmissionInvalidation` evidence record containing `bundle_sha256`, reason code, observed prior/current values or unavailable predicate, actor identity and observation sequence. The append receipt records operation path/hash/argv, canonical index before/after hashes and sequences, actor, zero charge/attempt effect and validation result; failure leaves `NOT_READY` with no mission-ledger write. The charge-boundary check re-reads the same canonical index, requires an append-only extension of the sealed prefix, rejects truncation/replacement/path/owner/sequence mismatch, and blocks if any extension names the bundle hash. Because an invalidation can still race the ledger write, that window remains part of the disclosed TOCTOU residual risk. The sealed `READY` bundle proves only a past observation and is never current-validity evidence by itself.

### 6.3 Bootstrap governance and accounting

The single explicit exception before the administrative ledger exists is one HUMAN-authorized `ADMIN_LEDGER_GENESIS` seed program; it is not bootstrap, migration or mission execution. Its unavoidable root of trust is narrowly named: the HUMAN supplies and hash-pins one pre-existing reviewed generic create-once/version-guarded `RootControlJournal` writer plus an exact initial empty-journal hash, initial genesis AIP scaffold bytes/hash and canonical Task Workspace scaffold bytes/hash. The HUMAN decision binds those roots, the dedicated genesis Working AIP ID/path/hash, canonical Workspace path, exact allowed authoring/staging/canonical/receipt paths, no network/install/product/mission-control effects, writer/verifier/reviewer/acceptance identities, exact route roles, stop conditions, and a finite pre-ledger ceiling covering every model call, tool invocation, file effect, retry, AIP `start|step`, Workspace-control update, independent review, lint, staged verification, finalization and closure verification. Before every genesis model call, tool invocation or file effect, the coordinator appends and validates a unique `GenesisEffectAuthorization` that binds pair/activity ID, actor/tool/model, expected source hashes, exact allowed call/effect, maximum units/outcomes and immediately debits the worst case from the finite seed ceiling. After observation it appends `GenesisEffectOutcome` with the authorization hash, result, actual before/after hashes and units; unused debit is not refunded. A crash/open pair is `UNKNOWN`, remains fully consumed and blocks every later activity until a fresh HUMAN recovery decision binds the open pair and observed state. Every recovery or replacement genesis carries forward all closed and open-pair conservative debits under one HUMAN-declared cross-replacement cumulative genesis ceiling; replacement never refills or resets consumed units. A HUMAN decision that increases that cumulative ceiling is a new explicit budget authorization disclosed in the WBS approval packet, not an implicit replacement effect; missing, duplicate or unlinked pairs block closure. The journal writer is the only unmetered primitive; its own append is create-once/atomic and fail-stop: if an expected root entry is not hash-equal and readable, no retry, administrative mutation or mission charge is allowed, and only a new HUMAN-authorized control-plane genesis may replace the whole inactive control plane. Its fixed overhead plus every authorized invocation and observed/UNKNOWN outcome remain visible in the bounded journal; a failed root append cannot silently permit later work. No staged contract/validator/append/fixture bytes or prior review evidence are presumed to pre-exist. Genesis keeps this journal active afterward under a separate finite `administrative_recovery` allocation, exact owner/tool/path/hash/sequence, and independent verification rules; it is used only to pre-record administrative-ledger append attempts and HUMAN-governed recovery, never ordinary work.

The first authorized effects create/re-read the exact AIP and Workspace scaffolds and invoke the declared `python3 .ai-work/tooling/run_aip.py start|step ...`; all runtime evidence remains in that Workspace. Within the finite seed ceiling, authoring produces candidate contract, validator, integrated append implementation, zero-use candidate and fixtures; independent review and scoped AIWS lint are mandatory and journaled. An attributable HUMAN byte-selection record then binds the exact candidate hashes. Only those selected bytes enter ordered staging writer → independent staged verifier → HUMAN stage acceptance → sequence-zero finalizer → independent canonical closure verifier. Each stage has exact post-selection argv/hash/paths and an immutable journaled receipt; the finalizer cannot overwrite differing bytes. Only the final HUMAN acceptance record binding the complete AIP/Workspace lineage, RootControlJournal prefix/hash, every genesis authorization/outcome pair including conservative UNKNOWN debits, authoring/review/lint evidence and all stage receipts makes sequence zero usable and returns only to `FROZEN`. It does not authorize `BOOTSTRAP_MAINTENANCE`; a separate exact HUMAN bootstrap decision must bind the accepted genesis receipt, bootstrap scope and allocation before that edge. Any missing/duplicate/unattributable activity, identity overlap, pre-existing differing byte, partial write, overrun or verification mismatch enters HUMAN-owned `ADMIN_LEDGER_GENESIS_BLOCKED`; only a fresh HUMAN recovery decision binding all staged/current hashes, the journal prefix and remaining/replaced finite seed ceiling may restore-or-complete. Genesis preserves zero mission attempts/charges and is never retroactively inserted into the new ledger; no other unmetered authoring, verification or self-creation is permitted.

Bootstrap implementation is not free or implicit work. It enters `BOOTSTRAP_MAINTENANCE` only after `ADMIN_LEDGER_GENESIS` and a verified/reviewed HUMAN-accepted migrated base have returned the ledger to `FROZEN`, through a separately HUMAN-authorized administrative task under its own Working AIP and Task Workspace; active/blocked migration and bootstrap are mutually exclusive. Before work, the HUMAN decision binds the objective, exact allowed write paths/effects, prohibited product/network/install/activation effects, one canonical **uncharged administrative resource ledger**, its resource/model-call ceiling, stop conditions, verifier, independent reviewer and HUMAN acceptance owner. Mission-attempt charging is prohibited because P15 and a valid mission ledger do not yet exist; there is no alternative accounting mode. Bootstrap entry and exit receipts re-hash the mission ledger and mechanically attest unchanged attempt set, unchanged `attempts_charged`, zero mission-attempt allocation/consumption and every administrative call/effect charged only to the HUMAN-bound administrative ledger. Any mismatch enters `BOOTSTRAP_BLOCKED`.

Before `BOOTSTRAP_MAINTENANCE`, `MIGRATION_MAINTENANCE` or any charged-accounting/review/transition-recovery state starts, a valid HUMAN-accepted `AdministrativeLedgerGenesisReceipt` must bind exact immutable `AdministrativeResourceLedgerContract` bytes/hash, canonical path/ledger ID, one **cumulative** administrative-ledger ceiling plus the separately finite fail-stop RootControlJournal `administrative_recovery` ceiling, with explicit state-specific ledger allocations for bootstrap, migration, the separately named `pre_charge_control_allocation` for effect 5.1(a) AIP pointer/ASC/Workspace-control transition, ordinary uncharged pre-charge admission/proof/evidence work (including prepare, capability/reviewer probes, bundle/intent sealing, invalidation and NOT_APPLIED completion), charged-accounting finalization/reconciliation, provenance-terminal recovery, review recovery and transition-intent finalization/recovery, deterministic validator, and coordinator-only version-guarded append operation. A post-charge state without its fresh HUMAN-bound allocation and sufficient remaining common ceiling remains blocked; it never consumes or creates a mission attempt. The same ledger ID/path/contract hash and cumulative ceiling must appear in both maintenance decisions, P15 and WBS-build entry evidence; a second ledger, divergent ceiling or unreconciled allocation is `BOOTSTRAP_BLOCKED`/`MIGRATION_BLOCKED` and later `NOT_READY`. Minimum common fields are: ledger/contract/mission IDs; sequence and previous-entry hash; attributable decision/invocation/actor IDs; operation/tool/model identity; resource units; exact allowed effect; state; cumulative usage; remaining ceiling; and entry hash. Every mutating operation is represented by a pair: a pre-effect `AdministrativeEffectAuthorization` binds deterministic pair/operation ID, expected source path/hash/version, exact argv/effect, fixed maximum units and allowed outcomes but makes no actual-result/after-hash claim; its post-effect `AdministrativeEffectOutcome` binds that authorization hash/ID, actual result, observed before/after artifact hashes/versions, operation receipt and consumed units. The validator recomputes canonical bytes, sequence/hash continuity, allowed-effect scope, cumulative arithmetic and ceiling. Reconciliation enumerates every authorized non-metering model/tool invocation and Workspace file effect against exactly one non-mutating entry or, for every mutating effect/attempt, one closed authorization/outcome pair; missing, open, duplicate, mismatched or unattributable activity fails closed. The ledger append primitive itself is the sole narrow outer-meter exemption: the HUMAN-bound contract pins its exact implementation/hash/argv/effect, an independently verified worst-case `append_overhead_reserve`, and a maximum append-invocation count. One atomic invocation may append exactly one activity record whose resource amount includes that fixed reserve; its operation receipt binds the activity invocation, before/after ledger hashes and actual success/failure without recursively generating another ledger entry. Every attempted append consumes one reserved overhead unit in reconciliation whether it succeeds or fails; failed/unknown append enters the blocked state, and each integrated ledger entry/hash must account for its invocation and append overhead within the bound; no separate receipt write exists. The contract, initial zero-use record, append capability and passing boundary fixtures must already validate before maintenance; they cannot be self-created or retroactively backfilled inside the state.

Every administrative-ledger append attempt is two-tier and never self-retries. First the coordinator appends a unique `AdministrativeAppendAttemptAuthorization` to the HUMAN-rooted `RootControlJournal`, debiting one attempt and worst-case overhead from its finite `administrative_recovery` allocation and binding ledger source hash/version, proposed semantic operation, attempt ID and candidate-construction rule. Candidate hashing is explicitly non-self-referential. Canonical `entry_body` excludes `entry_hash`, `raw_candidate_ledger_sha256` and every after-ledger hash; `entry_body_sha256 = SHA256(canonical(entry_body))`, and `entry_hash = SHA256(JCS([previous_entry_hash, entry_body_sha256]))`. The persisted entry contains `entry_body`, `entry_body_sha256` and `entry_hash` only. The ledger candidate embeds only the authorization entry hash/attempt ID, source raw-ledger hash and debited overhead. The full canonical candidate is then serialized and hashed as `raw_candidate_ledger_sha256`; before the ledger write, a second `AdministrativeAppendCandidateCommit` root entry binds the authorization hash/ID and that raw candidate hash, but its hash is not embedded back into the candidate. Only this valid authorization→candidate-commit pair permits exactly one version-guarded ledger invocation. `entry_hash` is the semantic receipt identity while the candidate-commit supplies the distinct raw persisted-candidate identity. Validators recompute both domains byte-for-byte and reject any field alias, missing pair or recursion. After the single invocation, the coordinator must create-once append an `AdministrativeAppendAttemptOutcome` to RootControlJournal binding authorization and candidate-commit hashes/IDs, observed current path/hash/version or unreadable fact, `APPLIED|NOT_APPLIED|UNKNOWN`, actual units, observation method/receipt and timestamp/sequence. Current==candidate records APPLIED; current==source records NOT_APPLIED and blocks rather than retrying; any other/unreadable state records UNKNOWN. Missing outcome remains fail-stop and cannot satisfy P14/P15, recovery or later append prerequisites; recovery consumes the durable outcome rather than inferring it. A retry requires a fresh HUMAN-permitted root attempt entry and a new ledger candidate/sequence that conservatively carries every prior failed-attempt debit before the semantic operation; exhaustion cannot retry. Any other hash/version or unreadable state is `ADMIN_LEDGER_APPEND_UNKNOWN_BLOCKED`.

Divergent/unreadable recovery is the sole post-genesis out-of-ledger mutation exception and is HUMAN-owned through the same root journal, never through the damaged ledger. A fresh HUMAN recovery decision binds exact source/candidate/current or unreadable evidence, all root-attempt entries, conservative worst-case debit, remaining root-recovery ceiling, exact version-guarded recovery argv/tool hash and one-way target (`RESTORED_BLOCKED` or terminal `ADMIN_LEDGER_UNRECOVERABLE`), verifier and final HUMAN acceptance. After a root-journal authorization, one recovery invocation may preserve corrupt bytes at a content-addressed evidence path and create an exact restored ledger generation at a new canonical path/generation; independent verification and a root-journal outcome bind before/after bytes. `RESTORED_BLOCKED` cannot resume until the restored ledger appends a recovery-lineage entry carrying all failed/UNKNOWN debits; terminal never resumes. Root-journal failure or ceiling exhaustion is permanently fail-stop. P15/P14 rejects any ledger whose generation, attempt set, debit sum, recovery lineage, verification or HUMAN acceptance is incomplete. Mandatory fixtures cover source-equal failure with multiple attempt debits, after-replacement APPLIED, divergent/unreadable recovery, root failure and exhaustion. Thus every ordinary ledger entry is its immutable append receipt; references elsewhere to a separate append receipt mean this embedded entry/hash and authorize no second file.

Effect 5.1(a), the AIP pointer/ASC transition, is a pre-admission control prerequisite funded only by the HUMAN-accepted `pre_charge_control_allocation`; its decision binds allocation ID, administrative-ledger ID/path/contract hash, exact AIP/ASC/Workspace-control argv and paths, maximum units, authorization/outcome pair IDs, ceiling reconciliation and expected before/after hashes. The allocation binds a finite `maximum_invocations`; each `FROZEN→PREPARING` invocation consumes a fresh never-reused authorization/outcome pair and direct-maintenance debit from that same allocation, including re-prepare after `NOT_READY` or `EXPIRED`. Exhausted invocation count or allocation enters `NOT_READY` and requires a fresh owner-rooted HUMAN replacement-allocation decision whose ceiling is still constrained by the unchanged cumulative administrative ceiling; no pair or debit is reused. It occurs before the admission cycle and is never debited again to a reservation. After that prerequisite and before every ordinary admission-cycle call/effect, effect 5.1(f) appends one immutable `AdmissionResourceReservation` as the first admission-cycle operation. It binds admission-cycle/mission/WBS/task/source IDs, exact permitted effect classes/argv hashes, deterministic non-mutating entry or mutating authorization/outcome pair IDs, worst-case units covering prepare/probes/review discovery, bundle/invalidation/intent/index OPEN/NOT_APPLIED or APPLIED completion/index CLOSE, guarded writer/receipt, every required accounting append/overhead and bounded recovery, maximum append count, expiry, prior ledger hash/sequence, and reserved units. The append deducts the full reservation from globally available cumulative ceiling under its single version guard; there is no refund, so later ledger growth cannot exhaust that already-deducted envelope. Each non-mutating operation appends its actual-usage entry immediately after the operation; each mutating operation appends its authorization before and its actual outcome after the effect. Append overhead is included as Section 6.3 already requires. The boundary aggregate binds immutable reservation ID/bytes/hash—not the mutable current ledger hash. The final guarded writer validates the current ledger is an append-only extension of the reservation prefix, every intervening entry has that reservation ID and an allowed effect, actual plus remaining bounded recovery units do not exceed reserved units, maximum append count is not exceeded, no competing/duplicate reservation exists for the cycle, and binds that pre-write reconciliation plus its deterministic pending authorization/outcome pair IDs in `GuardedWriteReceipt`.

Budget arithmetic is canonical integer arithmetic in one declared unit. Let `C` be the HUMAN administrative ceiling, `G` global committed units before a reservation, `R` its no-refund `reservation_debit`, `A` reservation actual/charged sub-usage, `O_admin_i` the administrative-ledger append overhead included once in operation `i`, `C_root` the independent RootControlJournal recovery ceiling, `U_root_j` the fixed operation/attempt unit debit for root append `j`, and `O_root_j` its separately priced append overhead. Root operation and overhead units are debited only from `C_root`; administrative/operation units only from `C`. The two ceilings are independent capacity gates, never duplicate charges for the same unit. Reservation append requires `0 < R <= C-G`, then sets `global_committed_units = G+R` and `global_remaining_units = C-(G+R)` exactly once. Later operation entries do **not** increment global committed again; they update only `A := A + operation_units_i + O_admin_i` and `reservation_remaining_units := R-A`, requiring `0 <= A <= R`. Failed or UNKNOWN/open operation pairs use their preauthorized maximum as `operation_units_i`; each administrative append overhead appears in exactly one `O_admin_i`, while each root authorization, candidate-commit, outcome or recovery append appears exactly once as `(U_root_j + O_root_j)` and decrements `root_remaining_units := C_root - Σ(U_root_j + O_root_j)`. Root records persist both fields plus cumulative root usage; failure/UNKNOWN consumes the preauthorized maximum and no retry can reuse `j`. Unused reservation units are never refunded, transferred or counted as use by another reservation. Direct non-reservation maintenance debits increment `G` once under their own allocation. Administrative entries store `C,G,R,A,O_admin_i,global_committed_units,global_remaining_units,reservation_remaining_units`; root records store `C_root,U_root_j,O_root_j,root_cumulative_usage,root_remaining_units`; the validator recomputes them and rejects negative values, double debit, hidden actual use, overrun, or a concurrent/conflicting reservation. Fixtures cover zero/partial/full use, failed/UNKNOWN, exact exhaustion, overhead, and concurrent rejection.

A normative worked fixture uses `C=100`, initial `G=20`, `R=50`, so reservation commit yields `global_committed=70`, `global_remaining=30`; operations `(units,overhead)=(10,2),(5,1),(UNKNOWN max 8,2)` yield `A=28`, `reservation_remaining=22` without changing global committed again. Independently `C_root=40` and root records `(U,O)=(3,1),(4,1),(5,1)` yield root usage `15` and remaining `25`. A further reservation of `31` fails global capacity; a root append costing `26` fails root capacity. Validators must reproduce these exact integers and reject double-debit, refund or cross-ceiling substitution.

Post-charge recovery arithmetic is separate and no-refund. Let `D` be one HUMAN-authorized recovery debit, `B` its charged sub-usage and `D_remaining=D-B`; let `G_current` be the exact validated persisted `global_committed_units` immediately before allocation, including the original `R` and every intervening reservation or direct maintenance debit. The allocation binds current ledger path/hash/version/sequence and requires `D>0`, `D<=C-G_current`, `B0 = allocation_write_units + O_admin_allocation <= D`, sufficient pre-existing `root_remaining_units` for its authorization/commit/outcome, and one root-authorized version-guarded ledger candidate that rejects any intervening change, atomically persists the allocation with `B:=B0`, updates `global_committed_units := G_current+D`, and sets `global_remaining_units := C-(G_current+D)` exactly once while leaving original `R,A` unchanged. Recovery operations update only `B := B + recovery_operation_units_i + O_admin_i`, require `0<=B<=D`, and charge preauthorized maximum on failure/UNKNOWN; unused D is never refunded/transferred. The allocation record persists `D,B,D_remaining`, both ceilings/remainers, original reservation/attempt/open-intent IDs and bounded fresh pair IDs. Fixtures cover zero/partial/full recovery use, allocation-append failure, UNKNOWN, exact exhaustion, root exhaustion and global exhaustion.

P15 sealing occurs only after the reservation and its root authorization/candidate-commit pair are closed. The bundle pins the resulting RootControlJournal prefix path/hash/sequence, remaining `administrative_recovery` ceiling, reservation ID and deterministic allowed attempt IDs/classes/count. P14 treats later journal state as valid only when it is a contiguous append-only extension of that prefix and every extension entry belongs to the same reservation, matches an allowed pre-bound attempt/pair/class and keeps actual plus worst-case remaining debit within both the reservation and root ceiling; unrelated, missing, reordered, duplicate, expired or excess entries expire the bundle. This P14 prefix rule governs only pre-charge readiness through the guarded write. After a charge it cannot authorize or reject recovery; P3/P15 alone permit one typed `PostChargeRecoveryAllocation` exceptional extension tied to the original reservation, same attempt/open intent, both remaining ceilings and terminal-reconciliation-only effects. It can never make a bundle READY again. Immediately before the guarded charge writer, after its own root authorization/candidate-commit and administrative authorization are appended, the coordinator re-reads the journal, recomputes exact debits/remaining ceiling and binds current root hash/sequence plus the reconciled prefix-extension proof into the post-invocation `GuardedWriteReceipt`; it never changes the sealed `ChargeBoundaryPreflightReceipt`. No sealed current-root hash is compared for literal equality after permitted growth.

The charge edge then follows a stricter crash-safe order. After `execution.json` is hash-equal and strict-valid, state is `CHARGED_ACCOUNTING_PENDING`; no APPLIED completion, index CLOSE, dispatch, dependency use or further ordinary transition is allowed. For the guarded write/receipt, APPLIED-completion persistence and index CLOSE, the intent pre-binds three deterministic authorization/outcome pair IDs. Before each effect the coordinator appends/validates its fixed contract-priced `AdministrativeEffectAuthorization`; after the effect (success, failure or observed unknown after restart) it appends/validates the matching `AdministrativeEffectOutcome` with actual before/after hashes, result and operation receipt. A failed attempted effect remains legitimately charged; an idempotent retry uses its own pre-bound bounded retry pair and may only reproduce the exact bytes. APPLIED completion is forbidden until the guarded-writer pair is closed; index CLOSE is forbidden until the completion pair is closed around the exact APPLIED completion; dispatch is forbidden until the index-close pair is closed around the exact CLOSE, every pair/append receipt validates, current ledger reconciliation passes within the reservation, and ordinary P3 proves zero open intents. A crash with an open authorization stays `CHARGED_ACCOUNTING_PENDING` or `COMPLETION_INDEX_CLOSE_PENDING`; recovery first records the observed outcome (including `UNKNOWN` with exact current hash), never predicts it, preserves the mission attempt/charge, and proceeds only under exact HUMAN-bounded recovery using remaining reserved units. Expected accounting appends after the boundary receipt do not invalidate it, but any unrelated entry, gap, open/duplicate/mismatched pair ID, failure or excess enters `ADMISSION_ACCOUNTING_BLOCKED`/`CHARGED_ACCOUNTING_BLOCKED`; exhaustion remains blocked until a fresh HUMAN `PostChargeRecoveryAllocation` record binds the original reservation, same attempt/open intent and current chain/index/root/admin hashes, a separate positive bounded `recovery_debit`, exact new authorization/outcome pair IDs/classes/count, terminal-only effects and no dispatch/acceptance. Its append atomically commits that debit once against `global_remaining_units` (`global_committed += recovery_debit`; original R/A unchanged); recovery sub-usage must not exceed it, failure/UNKNOWN charges preauthorized maximum, and P3/P15 require its closed root/admin pairs. The allocation can authorize only outcome observation, exceptional recovery completion/CLOSE and a guarded same-attempt transition to `FAILED_CHARGED_RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`; it cannot complete the original success path, dispatch or accept. Insufficient global or root-recovery capacity, or failure to publish the allocation, is terminal fail-stop preserving the charged/open/UNKNOWN evidence. No new admission cycle or attempt is created/reused.

Every bootstrap model/tool call, file effect, retry and review is recorded in that Workspace against the single cumulative ceiling. Any ceiling exhaustion, scope drift or missing accounting stops the task without making P15 pass. Only exact HUMAN-accepted schema/tool/operation bytes and hashes, with AIP/Workspace evidence, verification and independent review, may populate `bootstrap_authority`; bootstrap work cannot be hidden inside a successor WBS or silently consume its contingency reserve. Verified/reviewed exact bytes plus an attributable HUMAN acceptance return their hashes to `FROZEN`; failure, ceiling exhaustion or scope drift enters HUMAN-owned `BOOTSTRAP_BLOCKED`. A fresh HUMAN recovery decision must bind failure receipts, exact current/restore hashes, allowed effects and remaining/replaced cumulative ceiling before returning to `BOOTSTRAP_MAINTENANCE`; that decision, its receipt and final HUMAN byte acceptance append to the ordered `bootstrap_authority` lineage. Alternatively exact no-effect/restore proof plus HUMAN closure returns to `FROZEN` and is also appended. Otherwise it remains blocked and no improvised work is allowed. This design/review task specifies the requirement but does not authorize or implement the bootstrap.

### 7.1 Mission-specific contingency rule

The next WBS must reserve at least:

- one attempt for future plan-selection/reconciliation after a material reviewed correction; and
- one attempt for a runtime/tooling recovery that cannot reuse an implementation attempt.

The design recommends **two attempts** as the mission-specific contingency reserve. Only the HUMAN decision owner may select or change the operative amount; council output is advisory and cannot set it. The reserve cannot be silently borrowed by product tasks. Because the currently selected **WBS revision 32** (not this design's revision) has no slack, the later workflow is ordered: after reviewed design closure and an explicit HUMAN request to begin WBS work, an uncharged, inactive WBS candidate may be drafted and hashed; the HUMAN then records ceiling/reallocation, contingency and approval decisions bound to that exact candidate revision/SHA-256; only afterward may it be selected or admitted. Drafting is not selection, admission or execution. This current design task still does not authorize any WBS draft.

## 8. Semantic plan-closure review

Official schema validation remains mandatory but is insufficient. Before presenting a WBS for approval, a semantic closure matrix must answer for every task:

1. What exact state transition/deliverable is required?
2. Which actor/component owns it?
3. Is it produced by a declared command or permitted file operation?
4. For generated artifacts, is the canonical owning command declared?
5. Can every command run under the current backend without widening effects?
6. What negative path proves the control fails closed?
7. Who verifies, who reviews, who accepts, and are those roles non-conflicting?
8. What dependency evidence makes the task ready?
9. What happens on command denial, reviewer transport failure, crash after charge or hash drift?
10. Does the attempt and contingency arithmetic close?

A WBS cannot be labelled execution-ready while any row is incomplete, even if its format helper passes.

## 9. Ledger stabilization and migration

### 9.1 Freeze rule

The current `execution.json` remains frozen until a separate Working AIP and one canonical Task Workspace exist and an exact HUMAN administrative decision enters `MIGRATION_MAINTENANCE`. The AIP is stable macro-control; every migration runtime fact, mapping, call/effect, candidate, verifier/reviewer result and decision reference lives in that Workspace. Reporting or design work must not normalize the ledger in place, and ordinary admission cannot start while migration/recovery is active. Because P3 deliberately blocks charging any WBS attempt against the known strict-invalid ledger, this one-time migration is **uncharged control-plane maintenance performed before successor-WBS build**, not a product or WBS execution attempt and not acceptance evidence.

The HUMAN authorization must bind the Working AIP ID/hash, canonical Workspace path, current step/ASC, exact pre-migration ledger SHA-256/version, target contract hash, exact migration/restore argv or permitted version-guarded file operation, allowed write path, no-product-effect boundary, the exact Section 6.3 canonical administrative-ledger ID/path/contract hash and its single cumulative ceiling/allocation, stop conditions, verifier, reviewer and HUMAN acceptance owner; any source hash/version change before write invalidates the authorization and requires a new HUMAN decision. The migration operation and receipt must assert and mechanically verify: no attempt ID allocated or consumed, `attempts_charged` unchanged, attempt set unchanged, no AdmissionBundle sealed/consumed, no task/product mutation, and no acceptance transition. This authority cannot be reused for a charge write. For the first post-migration charge, P3/P14/P15 require the pre-charge source to equal the exact HUMAN-accepted migrated base bytes/hash/version and require the bundle `migration_authority` receipt to re-hash an ordered lineage of the initial decision, every recovery decision/receipt and final HUMAN base acceptance; every link must prove designated-owner/delegation identity, exact before/current/final ledger bindings, scope/effects, common administrative-ledger ceiling and non-revocation. For every later charge, P3 requires the complete ordered Section 9.1 `ExecutionTransitionIntent`/`ExecutionTransitionCompletion` pair chain from that base through each charge and ordinary status/outcome/review/acceptance candidate/persisted hash; only linked authorized state edges are accepted, while any missing/unlinked change or improper attempt/count mutation is `NOT_READY`. The coordinator is the migration-operation owner, a separate verifier and reviewer validate the candidate, and only the HUMAN accepts/selects migrated bytes. The state exits only to `FROZEN(new base)` after exact HUMAN acceptance of the candidate hash plus governed scoped AIP/Workspace lint and closure evidence, or through `MIGRATION_RECOVERY` to `FROZEN` after exact backup-equality proof; restore mismatch enters `MIGRATION_BLOCKED`. Every administrative resource/tool/model call and retry is recorded against the same Section 6.3 cumulative ledger/ceiling, never against a mission attempt.

A canonical append-only `TransitionIntentIndex` serializes the protocol without claiming cross-file atomicity. Before intent creation, ordinary P3 requires a closed chain and zero open intents; for non-charge edges, the boundary receipt binds prior index hash/sequence, logical intent ID, expected sequence and any acyclic precomputable path; for a charge edge it binds only prior index hash/sequence, `logical_intent_id`, `PlannedIntentBodyCommitment`, expected sequence and source/version, while final intent hash/path are derived later and bound only by final intent, OPEN and `GuardedWriteReceipt`. Ordered OPEN is: (1) create-once persist and validate the intent record, then (2) version-guard append its exact OPEN entry. If the intended path is absent or contains exact valid bytes after a crash, state is `INTENT_INDEX_OPEN_PENDING`: P3 scans the canonical intent-root manifest and index, blocks all charge/dispatch/dependency use, and permits exact idempotent OPEN recovery followed—while ledger still equals source—by pre-bound NOT_APPLIED completion/CLOSE. If the create-once intent path is present but partial, differing or unreadable, enter pre-charge `INTENT_CORRUPT_BLOCKED`; never overwrite, charge, OPEN or treat it as valid. Its sole recovery is a fresh owner-rooted HUMAN decision binding planned intent, exact corrupt bytes/hash/version or unreadable evidence, source-equal/no-charge proof, administrative allocation, content-addressed `IntentCorruptionTombstone`, and a deterministic new `TransitionIntentRootManifest` generation. The coordinator preserves the corrupt object, writes/verifies the tombstone and superseding manifest under authorization/outcome pairs, independently verifies that the new manifest accounts for every prior valid object plus the tombstone, and requires HUMAN byte acceptance before returning to FROZEN/re-prepare. If an OPEN was partially or wrongly appended, the same decision additionally binds a tombstone-based `RECOVERY_CLOSE` in a verified superseding index generation. P3 validates the full manifest/index generation lineage; tombstoned bytes are evidence, never OPEN/APPLIED. Any failure remains fail-stop. A valid OPEN enters `TRANSITION_IN_FLIGHT`. Only a transaction-local `P3_IN_FLIGHT` validator—and only for the exact P15-bound guarded writer or exact restart/finalize command—may accept it; it requires the index to equal the prior prefix plus exactly one unexpired source/version/candidate/reservation/boundary-bound intent whose previous-completion hash is the closed-chain head. It cannot report READY, authorize another prepare/intent/charge, satisfy a dependency or be consumed by any other writer.

Ordered CLOSE is: (3) create-once persist and validate the exact APPLIED/NOT_APPLIED completion, then (4) version-guard append the matching CLOSE entry. A crash after (3) is `COMPLETION_INDEX_CLOSE_PENDING`: P3 remains non-ordinary/in-flight and only the exact idempotent close recovery may append the already-bound CLOSE; index CLOSE without a record, unmatched non-corrupt IDs, or an index-only disagreement is `TRANSITION_RECOVERY_BLOCKED`; partial/differing NOT_APPLIED completion dispatches only to `UNCHARGED_COMPLETION_CORRUPT_BLOCKED`, while partial/differing charged APPLIED completion or its index generation dispatches only to `COMPLETION_CORRUPT_BLOCKED`. The record scan and index must reconcile one-to-one by path/hash/sequence at every validator entry. Only a validated CLOSE yields zero open intents and returns to ordinary P3. Multiple, stale, wrong-source, skipped-sequence or unmatched open intents block. These index writes and receipts are explicit parts of Section 5.1(d)/(e) and P15; no step infers or repairs them silently. Restart follows the deterministic source/candidate/divergent branches below and never ignores an intent record or OPEN entry.

Every generic `TRANSITION_RECOVERY_BLOCKED` cause has one closed disposition. A fresh owner-rooted HUMAN `INDEX_RECONCILIATION_DISPOSITION` binds the exact intent/completion/root-manifest/index generations, raw hashes/versions, mismatch class (`CLOSE_WITHOUT_RECORD`, `UNMATCHED_NONCORRUPT_ID`, or `INDEX_ONLY_DISAGREEMENT`), source/candidate/current ledger equality, preserved attempt/charge invariants, administrative allocation/pairs, verifier and terminal target. If independently verified canonical record bytes exist and ledger equality proves NOT_APPLIED, the coordinator creates a content-addressed reconciliation tombstone plus superseding index generation and matching `RECOVERY_CLOSE`, then appends a gap-free `IndexReconciliationRecovery` completion-chain link and returns to `FROZEN` with zero open intents. Otherwise the sole target is the same-attempt terminal `FAILED_CHARGED_PROVENANCE_UNKNOWN` through the already-defined HUMAN-bound terminal-recovery intent/write/completion/CLOSE path; if safe canonicalization cannot be proven it remains blocked. P3 and P15 admit only those two named edges, their authorization/outcome pairs and byte-exact fixtures; neither edge can dispatch, accept, reuse an attempt or change the preserved charge.

After the HUMAN-accepted migrated base exists, every subsequent ordinary or recovery `execution.json` mutation—charge/allocation, active/dispatched, interrupted/failed, verification outcome, review pending/result, revision-required, acceptance-pending/verifying/reject/accept, and each explicitly named recovery edge—uses one uniform two-record protocol. The one pre-base migration write is explicitly excluded: CP-05 owns it through the migration decision/operation/receipt/verification/review/HUMAN-acceptance lineage, and P3 accepts only the final accepted base hash as genesis for the later intent/completion chain. **Before any ledger write**, the coordinator create-once persists an immutable content-addressed `ExecutionTransitionIntent` that pins source bytes/hash/version, previous completion hash/sequence, deterministic intent/receipt IDs and idempotency key, exact state edge, attempt ID/count invariants, actor/authority, selected execution-contract mapping, argv/effect and strict-valid candidate bytes/hash; only a charge intent may increment the attempt set/count. The charge-preimage seal is the charge edge's intent under this same schema. The ledger then persists through one version-guarded write from the pinned source, re-hashes equal and strict-validates. For **every** edge, only the exact P15-bound guarded writer may produce one immutable hash-bound `GuardedWriteReceipt`; every `APPLIED` create-once `ExecutionTransitionCompletion` must bind and validate that receipt plus the intent, exact writer/tool/argv, edge-specific boundary/authority check set and result, source/candidate/persisted hashes and strict-validator results. Missing, mismatched or non-P15 writer receipt forbids APPLIED and enters `TRANSITION_RECOVERY_BLOCKED`. The next intent links this completion hash/sequence. The intent pre-binds deterministic administrative authorization/outcome pair IDs for guarded writer/receipt, completion attempt and index-close attempt. On a charge edge, the Section 6.3 ordering makes the closed writer pair a prerequisite to APPLIED completion and makes all three closed pairs plus validated CLOSE and zero-open ordinary P3 prerequisites to dispatch/dependency use; every recovery preserves the charge. The canonical candidate is never dispatchable, dependency-satisfying or the source of another ordinary transition merely because its APPLIED completion exists.

The only action after an incomplete intent is the exact idempotent restart/finalize command named by that intent. If ledger bytes still equal the pinned source, a non-charge edge may perform its edge-specific fresh checks and then either perform the one guarded write or create-once close `NOT_APPLIED`. A **charge-edge** source-equal restart never writes the old candidate: it create-once closes the old intent `NOT_APPLIED`, appends an invalidation when authorized, and runs fresh Section 5.2 preparation/boundary construction to create a new bundle as needed, new boundary receipt, new candidate hash and new charge intent before any guarded write. Reusing or modifying the old intent/candidate is invalid; unavailable observation or any drift writes no ledger bytes and requires re-prepare; if bytes equal the candidate, APPLIED finalization is permitted only when a pre-existing immutable `GuardedWriteReceipt` from the exact P15-bound writer validates and binds the intent ID, writer/tool/argv hash, edge-specific boundary checks and authority/revocation state, source version/hash, candidate hash, write result and persisted hash; the coordinator re-runs strict validation, validates the exact closed pre-bound writer authorization/outcome pair, opens the completion authorization, create-once finalizes the identical APPLIED completion and closes its actual-outcome record; it then opens the index-close authorization, idempotently closes the index, closes its outcome record and reconciles before ordinary P3 or dispatch. Candidate equality without that receipt—including crash before receipt persistence—or any receipt mismatch enters HUMAN-owned `TRANSITION_RECOVERY_BLOCKED` and must not create APPLIED. Its sole same-attempt exit is a fresh attributable HUMAN `UNKNOWN_WRITE_PROVENANCE` recovery decision binding the orphan intent/OPEN, exact source/candidate/current hashes and strict-validator output, preserved attempt ID/count/charge, missing/mismatched receipt fact, exact terminal `FAILED_CHARGED_PROVENANCE_UNKNOWN` candidate/hash, recovery argv/effects, common-ledger allocation, independent verifier/reviewer and HUMAN terminal acceptance. The coordinator first closes the orphan through a validator-accepted immutable `UNKNOWN_APPLIED_NO_RECEIPT` recovery link and matching version-guarded `RECOVERY_CLOSE` index entry under their exact Section 6.3 authorization/outcome pairs—neither is an APPLIED completion or acceptance evidence—which record independently verified candidate equality, missing provenance, charge preservation and the HUMAN decision; P3 permits the resulting zero-open state only as the source of the immediately bound terminal-recovery intent. It then creates a fresh recovery intent from those exact candidate bytes to the terminal candidate, performs one new P15-bound guarded write/receipt with the Section 6.3 authorization/outcome pairs, APPLIED completion and index CLOSE, without allocating or consuming another attempt. The exceptional P3 link is valid only as orphan intent/OPEN → HUMAN recovery decision → `UNKNOWN_APPLIED_NO_RECEIPT` link → terminal transition; it cannot authorize dispatch, resume, acceptance or reuse of the unknown write and all interim states remain frozen. Any failed validation, changed bytes, unavailable verifier/reviewer, revoked authority, accounting breach or recovery-write failure remains `TRANSITION_RECOVERY_BLOCKED`; only an absent completion path may retry the same create-once content-addressed completion bytes, while any present differing/partial/unreadable completion enters `COMPLETION_CORRUPT_BLOCKED` and cannot be overwritten or called APPLIED. If current ledger bytes equal neither source nor candidate, fail strict validation, or expose a partial/corrupt write, enter HUMAN-owned `TRANSITION_RECOVERY_BLOCKED_DIVERGENT`; the candidate-equality branch is inapplicable. Its sole exit is a fresh attributable HUMAN `DIVERGENT_CURRENT_TERMINAL_RECOVERY` decision binding the orphan intent/OPEN, exact source/candidate/observed-current bytes/hash/version and validator diagnostics, conservative preservation/consumption of the original attempt ID and charge (never decrement or allocate a new attempt), an exact strict-valid `FAILED_CHARGED_PROVENANCE_UNKNOWN` terminal candidate/hash, a dedicated version-guarded repair implementation/argv/effect whose source is that observed current hash/version, common-ledger allocation/pairs, independent verifier/reviewer and HUMAN terminal acceptance. Under exact authorization/outcome pairs, the coordinator appends an immutable `DIVERGENT_UNKNOWN_WRITE` exceptional link plus matching `RECOVERY_CLOSE` for the orphan, then creates a fresh recovery intent from the exact observed current bytes—not the old candidate—to the terminal candidate, performs the dedicated guarded repair write/receipt, APPLIED completion and CLOSE, and independently verifies strict-valid persisted equality. P3 accepts only orphan intent/OPEN → HUMAN divergent decision → `DIVERGENT_UNKNOWN_WRITE`/`RECOVERY_CLOSE` → observed-current-to-terminal recovery; it never authorizes dispatch, resume, acceptance or reuse. Any source drift, failed write/validation/review/accounting or revoked authority remains blocked. Never migrate, synthesize a receipt or return to active execution. APPLIED and NOT_APPLIED completions form one gap-free hash/sequence chain from the HUMAN-accepted migrated base; every non-charge edge preserves attempt set/count, and acceptance edges bind the P16 decision plus verification receipt. P3 accepts only this complete intent/completion chain with a validated exact guarded-writer receipt on every APPLIED link, plus explicitly bound recovery links; missing/duplicate/out-of-order records, an incomplete intent outside the exact transaction-local `P3_IN_FLIGHT` exception, unlinked ledger mutation or unauthorized count change is `NOT_READY`.

Completion and index writers must first pass crash fixtures proving create-once temp-write/fsync/atomic-publish behavior and version-guarded atomic index replacement; absent proof is P15 `NOT_READY`. A differing/partial completion whose intended disposition is pre-charge `NOT_APPLIED` enters `UNCHARGED_COMPLETION_CORRUPT_BLOCKED`, distinct from charged recovery. Its sole exit is an owner-rooted HUMAN `UNCHARGED_NOT_APPLIED_COMPLETION_RECOVERY` decision binding the valid intent/OPEN, exact corrupt bytes/evidence, source-equal proof, zero allocated/consumed attempt and unchanged charge count, a content-addressed `UnchargedCompletionCorruptionTombstone`, matching `RECOVERY_CLOSE`, administrative allocation/pairs, index generation if direct append is unsafe, verifier and HUMAN byte acceptance. No `GuardedWriteReceipt`, attempt or charge may be invented or required. The coordinator then create-once persists one `UnchargedNotAppliedCorruptionRecovery` exceptional lineage record binding the prior valid completion-chain head/hash/sequence, `sequence = prior+1`, intent/OPEN/tombstone/recovery-CLOSE hashes, exact source-equal proof, zero attempt allocation/consumption, unchanged attempt set/count/charge and accepted index generation. That record—never the corrupt completion—becomes the new completion-chain head; the matching `RECOVERY_CLOSE` references its hash, and every next intent must bind it as `previous_completion_hash/sequence`. The corrupt object/generation is preserved. P3/P15 accept this exact verified exceptional link only when index reconciliation yields zero open intents and gap-free `prior head → recovery record → next intent`; it returns to FROZEN/re-prepare, never APPLIED or dispatch. Fixtures prove missing/partial/differing NOT_APPLIED completion, zero-open closure, next-intent linkage and no lineage reset/gap. Residual differing/partial **charged APPLIED** completion recovery is separately explicit: a fresh HUMAN `CORRUPT_COMPLETION_TERMINAL_RECOVERY` decision binds intent/OPEN, intended completion bytes/hash/path, exact preserved corrupt bytes/hash/version or unreadable evidence, original guarded receipt and attempt/charge, exact separate content-addressed `CompletionCorruptionTombstone` bytes/path, terminal `FAILED_CHARGED_COMPLETION_CORRUPT` candidate, administrative allocation/pairs, verifier/reviewer and acceptance. `MIGRATION_BLOCKED` is owned by the HUMAN decision owner and is frozen against all ledger/product writes. ### 9.2 Migration protocol

0. Start/resume the separately approved Working AIP, verify one canonical Workspace/current-step/ASC binding, and stop on lint/control mismatch.
1. Copy exact pre-migration bytes to a new immutable evidence directory and record SHA-256.
2. Consume the exact HUMAN-approved P15 execution-representation decision and pin its single target: either proven existing `dsh-wbs-execution/1` JSON pointers plus strict-valid fixture, or the exact versioned extension contract/validator/migration bytes. No transformation starts before this choice; no double migration or invented v1 fields.
3. Inventory every incompatible field and classify it as lossless-known, conflicting or unrelated damage.
4. Transform only lossless-known fields using a reviewed deterministic mapping.
5. On duplicate/conflicting paths, missing provenance or ambiguous acceptance, stop; do not choose a value.
6. Preserve historical attempts, charges, output hashes, reviews and HUMAN decisions byte-for-byte or by reversible reference.
7. Serialize one immutable staged candidate without touching the canonical ledger; when the contract permits a separate current projection, include its exact staged bytes/path/hash, otherwise stage the single-file candidate and record that constraint.
8. Run strict validator and execution-backed progress rendering on the exact staged bytes, independently review the before/after mapping and no-loss proof, and run scoped governed lint over the migration AIP/Workspace; any failure stops before canonical mutation.
9. The designated HUMAN selects the exact staged candidate hash and issues a separate write authorization binding pinned canonical source hash/version, selected candidate raw hash, exact version-guarded argv/effect, administrative pairs/ceiling and restore path. Selection is not final byte acceptance.
10. Perform exactly one version-guarded canonical write from the pinned source; immediately re-read canonical bytes and require raw byte-for-byte/hash/size equality to the selected candidate plus strict validator PASS.
11. A separate verifier independently re-reads the persisted canonical path, recomputes equality and strict validation, and records attempt IDs, charge count and acceptance-reference preservation. Only then may the designated HUMAN issue final persisted-byte acceptance binding the write receipt and verifier evidence; only that record enters `FROZEN(new base)`.
12. Any write, re-read, equality, validation, verification or final-acceptance failure after step 10 enters `MIGRATION_BLOCKED` and uses only the pre-bound version-guarded restore. The restore receipt re-reads the canonical path and proves raw-byte hash/size/version equality to the immutable backup plus equality of attempt IDs, charge count and acceptance references. The known source may remain strict-invalid; that expected result is recorded separately and is not restore proof. Restore mismatch remains frozen/HUMAN-owned; exact restore permits no-selection return with a new candidate identity.

### 9.3 Migration acceptance

Migration passes only when:

- original bytes and hash remain available;
- every field has a deterministic before→after trace;
- charges and attempt identities are identical;
- accepted/interrupted/failed distinctions are unchanged;
- staged validation/review/lint pass, HUMAN selection precedes the sole canonical write, and persisted canonical bytes are independently re-read as raw-equal and strict-valid;
- final HUMAN persisted-byte acceptance binds the write and verifier receipts before `FROZEN(new base)`;
- no task becomes accepted or ready solely because of migration.

## 10. Generated and mutable control-artifact ownership policy

- AIP remains stable macro-control; runtime facts remain in Workspace.
- Every generated or mutable mission-control artifact has exactly one declared owner and write mechanism in the approved closure matrix: AIP pointer/ASC → exact `run_aip.py start|resume|step` argv; execution ledger/current projection → coordinator through the declared migration operation or the Section 9.1 uniform prevalidated, version-guarded `ExecutionTransitionIntent`/`ExecutionTransitionCompletion` pair protocol for every charge/status/outcome/review/acceptance edge; progress/report rendering → the official read-only renderer; AdmissionBundle computation → coordinator through the read-only prepare command; durable AdmissionBundle bytes → coordinator through the separate create-once content-addressed seal command; AdmissionInvalidation → coordinator through the bootstrap-pinned canonical index and exact uncharged append argv/effect (the invalidation-specific Section 5.1 exception); charge/boundary record → coordinator through the single version-guarded `execution.json` write bounded by HUMAN-approved WBS/effects; acceptance decision → exact decision-owner HUMAN or currently delegated acceptor whose actor.kind is HUMAN; non-HUMAN delegates prohibited; acceptance projection → coordinator through the closure-matrix-declared guarded operation/edit after candidate strict validation, with independent verification.
- Every WBS task requiring one of these transitions must declare the exact argv or permitted file operation, canonical path, effect scope and verifier. A migration projection may be written only by the migration operation named in the approved closure matrix.
- Manual or undeclared edits to ASC, execution/current projection, AdmissionBundle, charge record or report are invalid as acceptance/admission evidence and force `NOT_READY` until restored and re-verified.
- `status` can inspect but cannot satisfy a transition requirement.
- Freshness and raw-byte hash must be observed after the final authorized mutation for every control artifact; a pre-write observation cannot satisfy the post-write receipt.

## 11. Command and sandbox capability preflight

Capability preflight does not execute product behavior. It records only that, at the stated observation epoch:

- executable names and referenced files exist at canonical paths;
- cwd is readable and declared;
- required sandbox/effect mode is available;
- timeout and output evidence can be collected;
- no command resolves through a forbidden symlink or external package store;
- exact command is included in root/task unions.

A capability receipt must distinguish `available`, `denied`, `unavailable`, and `not_probed`. `not_probed` is not PASS. A later task-command failure remains a charged attempt outcome, but an already-known unavailable backend must never be discovered after charging.

## 12. Reviewer availability and fallback

Before charge:

1. Bind the immutable review policy/rubric and packet-template identity, required lanes, independence/model-family rules, HUMAN gate, evidence-path convention, typed retry policy and worst-case call budget. Do **not** require a final packet hash before output/verifier bytes exist.
2. Inspect live routes and record provider, model, route family and eligible replacement role for every primary/fallback; record availability as a snapshot, not a provider-capacity reservation.
3. Persist reviewer restrictions and a substitution matrix proving every allowed single-primary replacement preserves lane coverage, separate invocation identity and two-family internal diversity; otherwise admission is `NOT_READY`.

After implementation output and verifier evidence exist, but before review dispatch:

4. Construct the final immutable packet/manifest from exact output, verifier and policy/template bytes; persist and hash-pin those final bytes, re-check route/substitution eligibility, and bind their hashes to the charged attempt's review evidence.
5. A provider/transport/schema failure before valid verdict permits one typed retry or eligible fallback on **byte-identical final packet bytes** without a new implementation attempt. If no eligible route remains, enter `REVIEW_PENDING`; never substitute an unbound placeholder packet.
6. A valid adverse verdict is never retried to seek PASS; it requires disposition/revision.
7. Reviewer output remains advisory until the attributable HUMAN acceptance owner acts.
8. The coordinator may record/project an exact HUMAN decision but can never originate required acceptance. Any alternate acceptor must have actor.kind HUMAN and a complete hash-pinned, non-revoked, exact-scope delegation chain from the designated mission decision owner before admission; a task-declared rule cannot transfer that authority.

The review policy must name and raw-hash the exact accepted `dsh-council-review/references/contracts.md` and `risk-and-routing.md` bytes; their reviewer, outside-view and Chairman schemas/verdict rules are mandatory inputs, not prose conventions. A review is complete only when a deterministic validator emits an immutable `ReviewClosureReceipt` PASS bound to the exact final review packet/hash, artifact manifest, HUMAN-approved review-policy bytes/hash, expected and actual lane/invocation/result hashes, schema-validation results, the complete append-only `ReviewRouteAuthorizationAmendment` chain (or explicit empty-chain hash), required distinct route families, original aggregate call/retry ceiling, amendment slot assignments, typed-retry usage/dispositions, outside-view advisory separation, adjudication trigger/record or explicit not-triggered proof, separate Chairman identity/nonparticipation/result hash, final verdict and remaining budget. The validator proves every policy-required lane and gate is complete; one schema-valid lane or an unclosed Chairman response is never sufficient. Only `pass`/`pass_with_notes` receipt PASS may transition to `AWAITING_HUMAN_ACCEPTANCE`; `needs_revision` transitions to `REVISION_REQUIRED_CHARGED`, and blocked/insufficient/unavailable closure stays unaccepted in its declared state.

`REVIEW_PENDING` is a charged, unaccepted state owned procedurally by the coordinator. The exact final packet/hash, attempt/charge, output/verifier hashes, immutable review-policy/retry ceiling, total worst-case review-call reservation, used retry/call count and remaining unused retry slots/budget are immutable. The coordinator may perform only the already authorized bounded re-probe and retry/fallback; a freshly eligible bound route returns to `REVIEWING` on the identical packet. A `ReviewRouteAuthorizationAmendment` may only assign an exact replacement route to one still-unused original retry slot and remaining original reservation: it cannot add calls, budget, slots, amend the retry policy or follow exhaustion. At most one amendment exists per unused slot; when no slot/budget remains the only HUMAN choices are wait or terminal `REVIEW_UNAVAILABLE_CHARGED`.

Each amendment binds the designated mission decision-owner identity/source and exact raw HUMAN decision hash; its actor must equal that owner or be a HUMAN reached through a complete hash-pinned, non-revoked delegation chain rooted in that owner with exact task/review-route/retry-slot scope. It also binds prior policy and amendment hash/sequence, the consumed original slot ID and remaining aggregate count/budget, exact new provider/model/role/family, reason and typed retry disposition, eligibility and two-family/lane/independence proof, unchanged rubric/template/context/packet bytes, validator identity and administrative authorization/outcome entries. The complete owner/delegation chain and revocation state are freshly revalidated at amendment validation, immediately before dispatch and in `ReviewClosureReceipt`; every dispatch binds amendment hash/sequence/slot. Missing, unrooted, stale, conflicting, revoked, exhausted or packet/policy-changing amendment remains pending. Terminal `REVIEW_UNAVAILABLE_CHARGED` preserves the charge, leaves the task unaccepted and blocks dependents. HUMAN waiver cannot substitute for mandatory review or produce acceptance. Every review-state ledger write follows Section 9.1.

For high-risk design/governance reviews, use two independent internal lanes from distinct model families, a sanitized outside-view lane, conditional adjudication and a separate Chairman. Reserve worst-case **call budget** before dispatch; do not claim provider capacity is held unless a named provider primitive proves it. Record route loss and double-booking as residual risks.

## 13. Goal, Team and ownership synchronization

Admission requires a single snapshot proving:

```text
execution.coordinator == native Team Lead/coordinator
execution.active_work == native active workers/jobs for the mission
execution.run_state/pause-resume marker == runtime goal state/activation at the same observation epoch
goal.objective binds mission + WBS hash
```

Any mismatch blocks charge. A user message requesting continuation is authority evidence but does not substitute for the runtime goal reporting armed. If the runtime rejects resume, report the blocker and do not promise autonomous completion.

## 15. Verification strategy for this design

Council must challenge at least these scenarios:

1. valid WBS but invalid legacy ledger;
2. valid schema but missing ASC transition command;
3. reviewer disappears before verdict;
4. goal paused while execution ledger says active;
5. Team-plan revision changes after bundle creation;
6. sandbox disappears after capability receipt;
7. source hash changes between prepare and commit;
8. crash immediately after charge;
9. conflicting legacy hash entries during migration;
10. zero attempt slack;
11. coordinator is also acceptance owner while HUMAN review is required;
12. a report/helper claims PASS without execution-state closure;
13. invalidation append operation/index validation fails;
14. constructed or persisted charge-candidate ledger fails strict validation;
15. a reviewer substitution loses required lane/model-family diversity;
16. a delegated or other capable writer bypasses the procedural coordinator path;
17. an alternate acceptance owner lacks exact HUMAN delegation;
18. acceptance projection persists invalid bytes or its authority is revoked while pending;
19. a crash after charge leaves an interrupted attempt whose resume scope has drifted;
20. bundle sealing/persistence or post-seal retrieval fails;
21. migration restore bytes differ from the immutable backup;
22. ledger↔goal state or migrated-base/charge-lineage proof diverges;
23. one valid review lane exists but required lanes/diversity/Chairman closure do not;
24. acceptance verification is unavailable, inconclusive or refuted;
25. ledger write succeeds but transition-completion persistence crashes or fails;
26. multiple source-equal administrative append failures consume distinct root-journal attempts and exhaust correctly;
27. administrative ledger is divergent/unreadable and recovery root succeeds, fails or is exhausted;
28. completion or index publication leaves absent, partial or differing bytes;
29. genesis crashes after a call/effect but before outcome append, leaving an open authorization;
30. route amendment is unrooted, uses an exhausted slot or attempts to add call budget;
31. intent publication leaves absent, partial, differing or unreadable bytes before OPEN;
32. preflight passes but later root/admin/intent/index state changes before guarded-writer invocation;
33. persisted charge bytes are divergent/invalid while the original intent remains OPEN;
34. preflight zero-open index advances by anything other than its exact prebound one-OPEN entry;
35. an acceptance projection is partial, strict-invalid or divergent from both source and candidate;
36. a pre-charge NOT_APPLIED completion is missing, partial or differing, including unsafe index publication;
37. byte-exact construction fails the acyclic body-commitment→preflight→candidate→intent→OPEN derivation;
38. any removed acceptance-projection repair state/capability/allocation/entry criterion remains reachable;
39. uncharged completion corruption recovery does not become the exact next chain head or leaves an OPEN/gap.

A finding is material when it permits charge despite known unreadiness, loses/reuses a charge, changes acceptance, permits undeclared effects, or allows a non-HUMAN artifact to grant authority.

For every normative state/edge and ordinary or exceptional record—including intent/completion corruption tombstones, root/index supersession, `RECOVERY_CLOSE`, `UnchargedNotAppliedCorruptionRecovery`, `ChargedCompletionCorruptionRecovery`, `UNKNOWN_APPLIED_NO_RECEIPT`, `DIVERGENT_UNKNOWN_WRITE`, and `PostChargeRecoveryAllocation`—it names canonical path, schema/raw hash, deterministic validator, exact argv/effect, owner/authority, administrative/root accounting pair, failure disposition, P3/P15 acceptance rule and passing fixture. Missing manifest cells block drafting.

Drafting/hashing alone grants no selection, approval, admission or attempt authority. After exact candidate bytes exist, the HUMAN must bind the final ceiling/reallocation, contingency, WBS approval and allowed effects to that exact revision and SHA-256 before selection or admission; any later byte change requires a new binding.

The subsequent WBS must start with an uncharged admission proof followed by the first bound charge operation; product matrix/control/runner tasks remain blocked until that proof is accepted. Ledger migration and admission-mechanism bootstrap are pre-WBS control-plane prerequisites, not tasks hidden inside a WBS whose own P3/P15 could not yet pass.
